Every background is computed live, not picked from a fixed per-condition palette. This page runs the exact pipeline from app.js: a time-of-day base gradient (DYNAMIC_SKY) is tinted by a condition veil (WEATHER_VEIL), the result is hue-matched to a TINT_FAMILIES family, and its accent is deepened into the two colours that paint the CSS radial mesh.

Rows are conditions, columns are the six sky phases (cloud cover 50%). Click any cell to open it in the playground.

Base sky gradients DYNAMIC_SKY

The six time-of-day gradients, before any weather veil. The app interpolates between adjacent phases as the sun moves.

Condition veils WEATHER_VEIL

Each condition's tint colour, mix strength and darken amount, shown applied to the Day sky. Final stop = darken(lerp(sky, veil, mix), darken).

Tint families TINT_FAMILIES

The matched sky colour selects a family. Its dark-fg (dfg) is deepened into the actual mesh accent (rightmost swatch). Light bg/fg are used for tiles/icons in light contexts.
