Weather palette preview

Every background is computed live, not picked from a fixed per-condition palette. This page runs the exact pipeline from app.js. It is a two-layer model: the time of day (DYNAMIC_SKY) owns the mesh's hue and brightness so night always reads as night, while the condition (CONDITION_MOD) only modulates how vivid and how deep it is. Hues come from the matched TINT_FAMILIES family, painting the two colours of the CSS radial mesh.

Base sky gradients DYNAMIC_SKY

The six time-of-day gradients. These own the mesh hue and brightness. The app interpolates between adjacent phases as the sun moves.

Condition modulation CONDITION_MOD

Each condition is just two numbers: a saturation multiplier (how vivid) and a lightness delta (how deep). Shown here applied to the Day sky so you can compare, but the same modulation rides on every time of day.

Tint families TINT_FAMILIES

The matched sky colour selects a family; its hue paints the mesh accent (rightmost swatch, shown at a vivid sky). Light bg/fg are used for tiles/icons in light contexts.
